Study on the Impact and Mechanisms of Early Drying Environments on the Microstructure, Mechanical, and Shrinkage Properties of 3D Printed Concrete

Investigating how early drying conditions affect the performance of 3D printed concrete

2. Project Description

Concrete 3D printing technology employs a nozzle to extrude and layer concrete. This technology reduces labor and formwork expenses, increasing construction efficiency. However, the absence of formwork in post-printing stages exposes 3D printed concrete (3DPC) to dry conditions that affect moisture evaporation, cement hydration, and consequently, its mechanical and shrinkage properties. This research explores the effects of dry environments on the performance of 3DPC, focusing on its mechanical properties, shrinkage behavior, as well as water consumption and pore structure. These research findings contribute to a comprehensive understanding of the performance of 3DPC exposed to dry environments in its early stages, providing a research foundation for practical applications.
