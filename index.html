Biography

I am currently a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Toronto, Canada. My research background spans low-carbon cementitious materials and 3D printed concrete, with an emphasis on characterizing multi-scale properties, ranging from fresh-state rheological behaviour to hardened properties, including mechanical performance, shrinkage, durability, and sustainability.

My research interests include:

  • Topic 1 Low-Carbon Cementitious Materials
  • Topic 2 3D Concrete Printing
  • Topic 3 Multi-scale Material Characterization
